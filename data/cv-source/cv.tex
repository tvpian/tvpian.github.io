\section{Research Focus}
I want to build robots that understand the people they work with and keep learning from them over time. I take inspiration from how people learn: we build on experience, recognize what a new situation requires, identify gaps in what we know, and acquire and retain useful knowledge. I want to investigate how robot-learning architectures can accumulate and use contextual knowledge across tasks, people, and environments, so that acquiring one skill makes the next easier to learn. My work spans multimodal human understanding, learning from intervention, policy explainability and predictive models, with physical-robot, simulation and offline evaluations.

\section{Research Threads}
\thread{Learning from People}{PHYSICAL ROBOT AND SIMULATION}{How can robots improve from people's demonstrations and corrections without losing what they already do well?}
\begin{compactitems}
\item Built a physical-robot workflow that adds reviewed human takeover corrections to demonstration data before retraining and rolling out the next policy. \evidence{https://tvpian.github.io/\#continual-learning}{Overview}
\item Developing a portable egocentric interface to record people performing tasks themselves, using the teleoperation data pipeline. \evidence{https://tvpian.github.io/\#egocentric}{Overview}
\end{compactitems}
\thread{Policy Explainability and Representation}{SIMULATION AND PHYSICAL ROBOT}{What has a policy learned, and what shapes its actions?}
\begin{compactitems}
\item Used representation probes and activation patching in simulation to test whether encoded word-object associations influence actions, treating attribution maps as diagnostics rather than causal explanations. Across training setups and three seeds, the action head did not use the encoded associations. \evidence{https://tvpian.github.io/\#explainability}{Overview}
\item Tested instruction grounding on a real bimanual robot by scoring object choice separately from pick completion. Higher overall success did not imply better selection of the instructed object in these comparisons.
\item Tested what policies retain after privileged training inputs are withdrawn, comparing teacher-student training and learned auxiliary heads in simulation.
\end{compactitems}
\thread{Predictive Models, Inverse Dynamics, and Transfer}{OFFLINE AND SIMULATION}{How can predicted outcomes and inverse dynamics support action learning on physical robots?}
\begin{compactitems}
\item Trained image-based inverse dynamics in simulation to test whether actions inferred from video can replace recorded actions, using recorded-label and executed-command controls. \evidence{https://tvpian.github.io/\#world-models}{Overview}
\item Adapted a published video-action model to teleoperation data by retraining its action head, then evaluated it offline.
\item Fine-tuned a world-action model in simulation to assess predicted futures, instruction grounding and failure signals separately from task success.
\end{compactitems}
\thread{Understanding People}{PRIOR WORK | 2021-2024}{How can a robot recognize what people are doing and what they mean from incomplete, multimodal evidence?}
\begin{compactitems}
\item Co-developed \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11655}{Track Anything Rapter} for drone following from targets specified by click, box, image or text, using foundation-model perception and visual servoing.
\item Built an exploratory demonstration, speech and gesture policy on \href{https://tvpian.github.io/projects/natsgd/}{NatSGD (Snehesh Shrestha, UMD)}.
\item In a graduate course project, fused camera views for pose-based action recognition and fall detection under occlusion, raising F1 from 0.76 to 0.82. \evidence{https://tvpian.github.io/projects/multimodal-perception/}{PoseFusion}
\end{compactitems}
