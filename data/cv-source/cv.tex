\section{Research Focus}
I want to build robots that understand the people they work with and keep learning from them over time. My research takes inspiration from how people learn and understand one another: a robot should recognize what it does not yet know, learn it from the right person through demonstration, correction, or instruction, and keep what matters. I approach this through multimodal understanding of people, learning from human intervention, explainability of learned policies, and representation and predictive models, evaluated on physical robots.

\vspace{3pt}\textbf{Research interests:} human-inspired robot learning; multimodal understanding of people and human–robot interaction; learning from human demonstration, correction, and instruction; continual and interactive learning; explainability and representation learning for robot policies; vision-language-action policies; world and inverse-dynamics models; compliant manipulation; real-to-sim-to-real transfer; robot-policy evaluation.
