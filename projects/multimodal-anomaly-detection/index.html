How can physical motion, geometric sensing, and network behavior be encoded separately yet evaluated as one cyber-physical process?

For the U-ASTROD research program, I architected the multimodal sequential learning pipeline that processes LiDAR, odometry, and network traffic with modality-specific encoders before combining their representations for anomaly detection and reconstruction-based scoring.

Architecture

Do not force unlike signals through one encoder.

Each modality carries different semantics and temporal structure. The architecture first learns within-modality representations, then combines their evidence so physical and cyber anomalies can be examined together.

Research stages

Move from nominal behavior to attack evidence.

The research combines domain structure with data-driven representations instead of treating anomaly detection as an isolated classifier.

  1. 01

    Align heterogeneous sequences

    Robot state, range sensing, and network observations are synchronized so anomalous behavior can be studied across the physical and cyber layers.

  2. 02

    Encode each modality on its own terms

    Modality-specific spatiotemporal encoders preserve the structure of their inputs before fusion.

  3. 03

    Fuse learned evidence

    The learned representations are combined into a shared detector rather than reducing every signal to a hand-designed scalar.

  4. 04

    Use reconstruction as a diagnostic signal

    Feature-wise reconstruction error exposes which dimensions depart from learned nominal behavior.

  5. 05

    Evaluate on real robot attacks

    The system was tested on an autonomous cyber-physical platform rather than only on a synthetic tabular benchmark.
